# Brief: Alumwit

Kickoff 2026-11-10, scope and stack kickoff 2026-11-16. **Stack chosen, design not drawn, no code yet.** This repo
is public, so nothing in it names a real alumnus.

## Idea
Alumwit is a portal for alumni of Mahidol Wittayanusorn School (MWIT). Alumni find each other by batch (รุ่น), keep
a profile, and see news and events. It is unofficial until the alumni association or the school agrees to it.

A second goal: the owner can explain, maintain and hand over everything in it.

## Stack and limits
Checked 2026-11-16 on each provider's own pages unless a row says otherwise. Sized for 500 members.

| Part | Job | Free limit that matters | Against 500 members |
|---|---|---|---|
| Vercel Hobby | Runs the Next.js site | 100 GB data transfer, 1,000,000 function calls and 4 CPU-hours a month; scheduled jobs once a day at most. For "personal or non-commercial use". Over a limit, the site waits up to 30 days instead of billing (this line was read by the research worker, not re-read by Claude). | Far below. Open question 3 covers the non-commercial rule. |
| Supabase Free | Database, login, photos | 500 MB database, 1 GB files, 5 GB traffic, 50,000 monthly users, 2 projects. Paused after 1 week without use (restorable for 1 year). **No automatic backups.** | Far below: 500 photos at 200 KB each are about 100 MB (an estimate). The pause and the missing backups are handled below. |
| Supabase Pro, $25 a month | Backups from stage 3 | Daily backups kept 7 days; never paused. | Needed before real data, because stage 3 requires backups with a tested restore. |
| Cloudflare Access (free plan) | Email allow-list in stages 1 and 2 | 50 users. A visitor types an email and gets a 10-minute code; only listed emails get one. No account needed. | Enough for reviewers and 20 testers. |
| Resend Free | Sends invites and sign-in links from stage 3 | 100 emails a day, 3,000 a month, 3 domains; needs a domain the owner controls. | Enough if invites go out in batches under 100 a day. Needed because Supabase's own sender mails only the project's team, 2 an hour. |

Choices Claude made (technical, no change to what visitors see):
- **Stay movable.** Use plain Postgres and standard Next.js that also runs on an ordinary server. Supabase is open
  source and can be self-hosted, so the data and login can move to another server. Avoid features that work only
  on Vercel or only on Supabase's cloud; where one is needed, the pull request says so.
- **Keep the free database awake** in stages 1 and 2 with one scheduled daily request from the site.
- **Close the side doors:** Vercel's free "Standard Protection" locks the `*.vercel.app` addresses, so only the
  Cloudflare-guarded address is open. Cloudflare's proxy runs in "Full (strict)" mode.
- **Two Access rules never to add** (either lets everyone in, per Cloudflare's docs): Include "Everyone", and Include
  "Login Methods: One-time PIN". The rule is a list of email addresses.
- **Invite-only login** uses Supabase's switch "Allow new users to sign up", turned off.
- Vercel's own protection is not used for visitors: on Hobby it needs each visitor to have a Vercel account, and
  password protection costs $20 a project a month on the paid plan.

New accounts are the owner's steps: Vercel, Supabase (region Singapore, which can't be changed later), Resend at
stage 3. Cloudflare is already in use.

Thailand's personal data law (PDPA), from law-firm summaries; the regulator's own site refused our reads:
- Storing members' data abroad is allowed with their consent after they are told where it goes. No list of
  approved countries exists yet. Supabase offers a data processing agreement (version 1, 1 Aug 2026).
- A leak must be reported to the regulator within 72 hours where feasible, and to the members when the risk is high.
- A data protection officer is required from 100,000 people's data or for sensitive data: not our case.
- Small and non-profit organisations are excused from keeping a processing record, unless the processing is risky
  or not occasional. Whether Alumwit qualifies is not settled (open question 4).

Not checked: where Supabase keeps logs and backups (its agreement doesn't say; its support staff can reach data
from other countries); whether Cloudflare's 50 counts per account; whether someone can reach the Vercel site around
Cloudflare Access (open question 2).

## Features and tabs
Approved by the owner 16 Nov. A tab is a top-level page in the menu. The profile fields come from the September
notes.

| Tab | What it is for | Essential in v1 | First stage |
|---|---|---|---|
| Directory | Find alumni | Search by name or nickname. Filter by batch, country or city, and industry. Each result shows name, nickname, batch, city and work, as far as that person's visibility allows. | 1 |
| Profile | One person's page | Name, nickname, batch, graduation year, city, country, company, job title, industry, short bio, photo, links, and the visibility setting. Editing your own profile arrives with login. | 1 to view, 3 to edit |
| Feed (home) | News | Posts, newest first, each with a title, date, text and one picture. Members write posts from stage 3; a member's post shows after an admin approves it. | 1 to view, 3 to post |
| Events | Meet-ups and reunions | Upcoming and past lists. Each event has a title, date and time, place or link, description and an "I'm going" button. | 1 to view, 3 to keep answers |
| Admin | Run the site | Approve, invite and ban members. Approve or reject members' posts. Write and remove posts and events. A log of who changed what. Admins only. | 3 |
| Join / Sign in | Get in | Ask to join or use an invite, then wait for approval. | 3 |
| About and privacy | Trust | What the site is, who runs it and whether it is official. The privacy notice, where the data is stored, how to delete your account, and a contact. | 1 |

On every screen: the Thai/English switch, and a clear state for nothing to show, loading, failure and very long
text (names in both Thai and English).
